01 About

I look for the story hiding in the numbers.

I'm a Computer Science graduate from San Diego State University (Magna Cum Laude), now pursuing my M.S. in Big Data Analytics at SDSU. I work in Python for data manipulation, SQL for querying, and Tableau for visualization.

My approach is methodical: the best insights come from asking the right questions and letting the data tell its story.

Growing up in San Diego, I've been shaped by this city's diversity and its challenges in healthcare access. Working at an independent pharmacy showed me how data can improve people's lives, from spotting cost-saving patterns in prescriptions to streamlining the processes behind patient care. That's what pulled me toward data: seeing how the right numbers, presented the right way, change what people decide to do next.
